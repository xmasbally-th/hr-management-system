## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Deterministic scan:** 16 findings (12 warning, 4 advisory). Dominant family = AI/gradient palette (9) concentrated in the 4 role dashboards, navbar, sidebar, login. 1 gradient-text (hard ban) in login. 3 globals.css rgba advisories (acceptable utility colors). 1 broken-image = FALSE POSITIVE (word `<img>` in a JSDoc comment). 1 sidebar width transition (acceptable tradeoff).

**LLM assessment:** The component library and token system are genuinely clean and on-brand. The drift is localized: (a) the 4 role dashboards hardcode `text-slate-*` instead of semantic tokens — breaks dark/soft/bold themes — and decorate avatars with violet gradients (off-brand; brand hue is indigo); (b) the login page is a consumer-SaaS marketing hero (gradient text, glassmorphism, purple, "Enterprise Edition · v2.4") that contradicts the calm/institutional PRODUCT.md personality.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Dashboards don't respect the theme system.** 56 hardcoded `text-slate-*`/`gray` colors in `app/dashboard/_components/*-dashboard.tsx`. In dark/soft/bold themes the text stays slate-900, breaking contrast and theming. Fix: swap to `text-foreground` / `text-muted-foreground` / `text-primary`. Command: /impeccable colorize (token remap) then /impeccable audit.
- **[P1] Gradient text on login (absolute ban).** `bg-clip-text` + `from-indigo-300 via-purple-300` on the H1. Fix: solid white heading, emphasis by weight/size. Command: /impeccable quieter.
- **[P2] Off-brand violet gradient avatars.** manager-dashboard (×2), navbar, sidebar, avatar-upload use `from-violet-*`/decorative indigo gradients. Violates the One Accent Rule. Fix: flat Civic Indigo or neutral initials chips. Command: /impeccable quieter.
- **[P2] Login page tonal mismatch.** Glassmorphism, purple feature pills, marketing copy contradict "calm, trustworthy, institutional". Fix: quiet the hero to a calm institutional split-screen. Command: /impeccable quieter or /impeccable critique app/(auth)/login for a focused pass.
- **[P3] globals.css literal rgba + 3px radius** outside DESIGN.md. Minor; document or tokenize. Command: /impeccable polish.
